How to play

Aim & launch — Press and drag anywhere, then release. Pull back like a slingshot: the further you pull, the faster you fly. The dotted line shows your predicted path.

Gravity — Planets bend your flight. Skim a planet to slingshot around it — or crash into it. The dashed ring shows each planet's pull.

Goal — Collect every star shard ✦, then fly into the gate. The gate only opens when all shards are collected.

Combo — Collect shards quickly in a row to multiply points up to x5.

Perfect Orbit — Loop a full circle around a planet for +250.

Launches — Each level gives you a few launches. Crash, fly off-screen, or run out of time and you lose one. Unused launches are worth +150 each.

Keys — R restart · P pause · M mute. Touch works too.
