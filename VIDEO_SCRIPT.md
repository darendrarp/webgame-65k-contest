# STARSLING — Demo Video Script (< 3 minutes, no face)

**Purpose:** a short gameplay demo for the contest submission / Launchpad
listing / sharing to gather community upvotes. No face on camera — screen
recording + text overlays + the game's own synthesized sound.
**Target length:** 2:00–2:30. **Format:** 1080p screen capture (OBS or the
browser's built-in recorder), game sound on, no voiceover needed (optional
short voiceover lines are marked VO).

> Record against the **Base44 build** once published (that is the submitted
> game). The reference demo can stand in for a first cut, but re-record on
> the Base44 URL before sharing widely.

## Recording tips

- Record at 1920×1080, game in fullscreen browser (F11), hide bookmarks bar.
- Capture game audio (the synthesized chimes sell the polish). Keep the
  mute OFF; record in a quiet room if adding VO.
- Do 2–3 takes of the slingshot shots; use the cleanest. The trajectory
  preview is the star — linger on the aim for a beat before releasing.
- No face, no webcam, no copyrighted music — game sound only.
- Export H.264 MP4, < 100 MB if the platform caps uploads. Upload as
  Unlisted/Public on YouTube if a link is needed (the contest form may not
  require a video at all — check the form; the video is mainly for
  Launchpad + sharing for community votes).
